## 关键词

- Pullback: Minimum Requirement（Slide 2, 00:59，source-explicit，课件原文截图）：
  - Smallest reversal:
    - In bull, low of bar below low of prior bar
    - In bear, high of bar above high of prior bar
  - 图表左侧下跌走势中一根 bar 被红色矩形标出，对应 bear trend 里的 pullback（该 bar 高点高于前一根 bar 高点）
  - 图表右侧上涨走势中一根 bar 被绿色矩形标出，对应 bull trend 里的 pullback（该 bar 低点低于前一根 bar 低点）

- Implied Pullback: In Bull Trend（Slide 3, 03:06，source-explicit，课件原文截图）：
  - In bull trend, a doji bar or bear bar, even if not below low of prior bar, is an implied pullback (PB)
  - These are actual pullbacks on lower time frames
  - Always pay attention to pullbacks（source-explicit，讲解语音中提到，画面文字未直接显示，时间戳未确认）
  - 图表左侧（5 分钟图）上涨走势中一根小实体阴线被绿色矩形标出，箭头指向并标注：5 min bear bar is implied pullback

- Implied Pullback: In Bear Trend（Slide 4, 04:10，source-explicit，课件原文截图）：
  - In bear trend, a doji bar or bull bar, even if not above high of prior bar, is an implied pullback (PB)
  - These are actual PBs on lower time frames
  - 图表左侧（5 分钟图）下跌走势中一根小实体阴线（bear doji）被粉色矩形标出，内有蓝色圆点，下方红色箭头指向，标注：5 min bear doji is implied PB
  - 图表右侧（1 分钟图）下跌走势中若干根 bar 被粉色矩形标出（一段小幅回升），标注：It is an actual PB on 1 min chart

- Strong Trend: Why Does It Pull Back?（Slide 7, 10:06，source-explicit，课件原文截图）：
  - As trend continues, it gets far from stop / Risk is greater
  - Institutions must prevent risk from getting too big / Popular way is to reduce position size by taking profits
  - When bulls take profits, market pulls back, and goes sideways to down
  - Once bulls confident that bears will fail to reverse bull trend, bulls buy again
  - 图表底部标注一条橙色 "Stop" 水平线；上涨走势中依次用绿色/蓝色矩形交替标出多段：绿色为上涨段，蓝色为回调/横盘段

- 50% Pullback: Most Important（Slide 9, 15:19，source-explicit，课件原文截图，替换了之前 14:20 不完整的版本）：
  - 50% PB in bear means distance to stop (risk) is same distance as to bottom of bear trend (minimum reward)
  - Probability favors trend resumption, so traders like to sell on 50% PB in bear trend
  - Traders buy 50% PB in bull trend
  - Basis for much of math for traders
  - See videos on Measured Moves, and on Trader's Equation and Probability
  - 图表标出一段下跌走势：顶部、底部各有一条红色水平线，中间 50% 回调位置也有一条红色水平线；下跌走势后接续一段上涨走势

- Bar Counting: Help Find PB Entries（Slide 12, 20:48，source-explicit，课件原文截图，替换了之前仅含文字、不包含图表的版本）：
  - Method to help traders find PB entries
  - Trend often resumes after PB that has just one leg
  - Traders often wait for PB with 2 or 3 legs
  - Rarely, 4 – 6 legs: 2 – 3 leg PB can fail / 2nd PB of 2 – 3 legs
  - 图表展示一段先上涨后下跌再上涨的完整 K 线走势；图中用红色圆圈标出三个位置，下方分别用红色数字 1、2、3 标注（即 High 1 / High 2 / High 3）；右侧回弹后的上涨段用绿色数字 1、2 标注；文字标注 "Bulls will buy BO above High 3 Bull Flag" 与 "High 4 and big High 2"

- Bar Counting: Count Number of Legs in PB（Slide 13, 24:28，source-explicit，课件原文截图）：
  - For example:
  - An ABC correction has two legs

- PB in Bear Ends: Bar Goes below Low of Prior Bar（Slide 15, 27:24，source-explicit，课件原文截图）：
