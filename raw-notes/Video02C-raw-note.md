  - Traders simply, and painfully discover that price is wrong!
  - Market quickly moves to find new range
  - 图示：图表先在一段区间内震荡整理，随后出现连续阳线向上突破并快速上涨，图上未见额外指向标注

- Market Inertia: 80% Chance of More of the Same Behavior（Slide 号/时间戳未显示，source-explicit，课件原文）：
  - Markets tend to do what they've been doing
  - Look to the left to see what is likely to the right
  - Much more to bottom than candlestick pattern
  - Bear trend, so lower prices more likely than higher prices
  - Don't look to buy minor reversals

- Trading: Difficult（Slide 号/时间戳未显示，source-explicit，课件原文）：
  - Traders quickly learn that they lose money
  - No matter how hard they try to trade well
  - No matter how well they know candlestick patterns
  - Nice candlestick tops, but the bull continued up!
  - Context (the bars to the left), is more important than a candlestick pattern

- What Do Traders Repeatedly Talk About?（Slide 号/时间戳未显示，source-explicit，课件原文）：

- Two Forces Control All Markets: Context and Momentum（Slide 号/时间戳未显示，source-explicit，课件原文）：
